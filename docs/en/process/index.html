How we work

Five steps — from the task to launch. Scope and timelines are fixed after we discuss the project.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We discuss the task, goals and constraints, and clarify the expected result.

  2. 02

    Scope & plan

    We fix the scope, stages and timelines and agree them with you.

  3. 03

    Design

    We prepare the structure and interface, show a prototype and apply your feedback.

  4. 04

    Development

    We build the product, connect data and integrations, and test every feature.

  5. 05

    Launch

    We publish it, check key scenarios and hand over access with instructions.

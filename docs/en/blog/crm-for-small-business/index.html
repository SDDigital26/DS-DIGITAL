CRM for small business: why it matters

CRM for small business becomes necessary when customer data no longer fits in a spreadsheet and your head. Leads get lost, nobody remembers who returns the vehicle and when, and revenue is counted manually. At DS DIGITAL we build CRM systems tailored to company processes — see CRM Development.

Signs it is time to adopt CRM

  • leads arrive from several channels and not all are recorded;
  • repeat enquiries require a long search through history;
  • finances and rentals are tracked in different files;
  • staff spend time filling in spreadsheets.

What CRM delivers: the RIDEFLOW example

Our real project — RIDEFLOW: a CRM for rental management. It includes an occupancy calendar, payments and deposits, contracts with guest e-signature, analytics and profit, GPS vehicle tracking and a Telegram booking bot. This is not a presentation — the site publishes interface screenshots on demo data.

How to choose a CRM

First describe the process: what you sell, how leads arrive, what data must be stored. Then compare off-the-shelf solutions with custom development. A ready-made product is faster, but may not fit your processes — then we build a system around the task.

Integrations

A CRM is useful only when data flows into it automatically. We connect site forms, email and Telegram notifications, and external services via API. See API integrations.

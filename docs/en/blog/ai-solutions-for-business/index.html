AI solutions for business: where they actually help

Where AI is useful and where plain automation is enough. An honest breakdown with no promises or invented numbers.

21 November 2026

AI solutions for business: no magic, no promises

AI solutions for business are discussed everywhere, but what matters to an owner is simple: what it delivers and what it costs. At DS DIGITAL we treat AI as a tool — see AI Solutions. We do not promise to replace staff or publish invented numbers.

Where AI genuinely helps

AI helps when there is a lot of repetitive information to process: lead classification, draft replies, document search, helping a client pick a service. All of this can be wired into your processes and forms.

Where automation is enough

If the task follows fixed rules — for example, a form submission must enter the CRM and trigger a Telegram notification — a language model is unnecessary. Business automation handles it more reliably.

Our approach to the AI assistant

The DS DIGITAL site has an AI assistant. By default it runs a free step-by-step script with no external services. Optionally, an admin can connect a provider via API — for example Google Gemini or your own OpenAI-compatible server. Consent, steps and request creation happen on the server; the model may only rephrase replies and never quotes prices or deadlines.

Safety and honesty

We do not send data to an external API before user consent. If the provider is unavailable, the assistant falls back to the script. That lets you use AI without risking leads.

How to start

Describe the task and the data involved. We will help choose: a script, AI or a plain integration. For an estimate use the cost calculator; to discuss — contact us.

FAQ

Does AI replace staff?

No, we treat AI as a tool for routine tasks: classification, reply drafts and helping clients choose.

Do I need AI to connect a form to a CRM?

Usually not — rule-based automation is enough. AI makes sense for unstructured information.

Do you send data to third-party APIs?

Only after user consent and only with a connected provider. Before consent, a local script runs.

How do I discuss an AI task?

Contact us with a task description — we will advise whether a script, AI or an integration fits.

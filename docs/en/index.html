About the studio

DS DIGITAL is a digital development studio: we build products, automate processes and launch things that bring leads.

We work with businesses that need a working tool rather than “a website for the sake of a website”: a site that brings clients, an app that simplifies work, or automation that removes routine.

We take the task end to end: discuss requirements, design, develop, test and launch. We show intermediate results at every stage so feedback does not pile up at the end.

We do not promise what cannot be verified: scope, timelines and price are discussed before the start and fixed in our agreements.

How we work

Five steps — from the task to launch. Scope and timelines are fixed after we discuss the project.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We discuss the task, goals and constraints, and clarify the expected result.

  2. 02

    Scope & plan

    We fix the scope, stages and timelines and agree them with you.

  3. 03

    Design

    We prepare the structure and interface, show a prototype and apply your feedback.

  4. 04

    Development

    We build the product, connect data and integrations, and test every feature.

  5. 05

    Launch

    We publish it, check key scenarios and hand over access with instructions.

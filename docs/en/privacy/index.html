Privacy policy

How we process the data you leave on this site.

What data we collect

Data you enter in the request form: name, contact (Telegram, phone or email), task description and the selected service. We also store technical details: the page the request came from and the consent date.

Why we need it

Only to contact you about your request and discuss the task. We do not sell this data or share it with third parties for advertising.

Legal basis

Your consent, confirmed by the checkbox before sending the form. Without consent the request is not sent.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to work on the request and meet legal requirements. You can ask us to delete your data via the contact form.

Your rights

You can request access to your data, correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent to processing.

Cookies

Cookie use is described in the separate Cookie policy. Only essential cookies work before your consent.

For questions about data or terms, write to us via the form on the main page.
